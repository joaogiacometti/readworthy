The feud between two celebrity chefs took another turn this week. After one called the other's new restaurant "a theme park with tablecloths" on a podcast, the other fired back on social media, calling their rival "a has-been who hasn't cooked a meal in ten years."

Fans quickly picked sides, with thousands of comments under each post. Staff from both kitchens reportedly joined in. Neither chef has responded to requests for comment, but insiders say the two haven't spoken since a falling-out over a shared cookbook deal years ago.
