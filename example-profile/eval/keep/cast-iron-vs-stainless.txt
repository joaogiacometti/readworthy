Cast iron vs stainless: we measured both

We heated a 26 cm cast-iron skillet and a tri-ply stainless pan of the same size on the same burner and measured the surface with an infrared thermometer. Stainless reached 200°C in about 3 minutes, cast iron in about 6, and the cast iron heated less evenly: the center ran 40°C hotter than the rim.

Cast iron's advantage showed when food went in. Four cold chicken thighs dropped the stainless pan by about 70°C, and the cast iron by about 35°C, because its heavier mass stores more heat. The thighs in cast iron browned noticeably better. For fast, responsive cooking, like sauces or sautéing vegetables, the stainless pan was easier to control. For searing and shallow frying, the cast iron won.
